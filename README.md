# 🛡️ EdgeGuard AI: Local Laptop Privacy Shield

Tired of feeling like someone is reading your screen at the coffee shop? Worried about leaving your laptop unattended? 

EdgeGuard is an intelligent, locally hosted Edge-AI privacy shield. Using advanced computer vision, it monitors your background and instantly neutralizes visual hacking threats before they can steal your data.

## 🔥 Key Features
* **Cafe Panic Mode:** If a bystander walks up behind you, EdgeGuard intercepts your OS to instantly minimize all your open windows (`Win + D`) and silently purges your clipboard RAM.
* **Zero-Trust Auto-Lock:** EdgeGuard detects when you step away from your desk and automatically throws up the Windows Lock Screen to secure your workstation.
* **Smart Background Obfuscation:** Holds up a phone camera or a sensitive document? EdgeGuard applies a targeted pixel mask directly over the threat.
* **100% Offline & Private:** Powered by an ONNX-compiled Edge AI engine. Zero cloud processing. Your video feed never leaves your local machine.
* **Everyday Modes:** Includes Battery Saver throttling for unplugged sessions and a Silent Mode for quiet environments like libraries.
